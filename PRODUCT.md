# UniPathway product brief

**Primary users:** Pakistan-based learners and parents, admissions agents, tutors, lecturers, centre administrators, employers and international partners.

**Core purpose:** Education admissions, qualifications, tuition, pathways, language learning, LMS and employer placement journeys.

**Interface constraint:** Separate public marketing from role-guarded dashboards. Disclose course levels and entry requirements, fees and funding conditions clearly. Follow year-group/Level 3 through higher qualification progression, inclusive Pakistan imagery, no fabricated employer endorsements, and clear assessment outcomes. Make forms usable on affordable Android devices.

**Source of truth:** this repository's feature code, legal/security/role requirements and existing documentation; do not infer active integrations, permissions or service-level guarantees from a mockup. Changes in this initiative may improve UX but do not automatically deploy or change backend contracts.
