# Residential Academic Weeks

Applicable OTHM/Qualifi and other configured programmes have two in-person Islamabad residential academic weeks per academic year: one in each semester. Each runs Sunday afternoon arrival to the following Sunday morning departure.

## Semester 1 — Integration, teaching and experience
Primary emphasis: induction/community, intensive teaching, workshops, subject labs, tutorials, lecturer contact, university/careers guidance, personal guidance, social/networking and up to two structured course-related work-experience days where appropriate.

## Semester 2 — Consolidation, assessment and progression
Primary emphasis: revision/consolidation, advanced workshops/labs, tutorials, assessment preparation and scheduled controlled examinations/assessments where the programme's approved assessment strategy permits them. Also include progression planning, university/careers appointments, employer engagement and a closing academic/community programme. Work experience can be reduced/replaced by assessment activity where required.

## Example rhythm
Sunday PM arrival/registration/welcome.
Monday-Tuesday intensive academic teaching/workshops/labs.
Wednesday-Thursday Semester 1: structured placements; Semester 2: revision/exams/assessment workshops according to programme.
Friday tutorials/academic support/labs.
Saturday progression expo, careers/employer activity, guidance, academic catch-up and closing event.
Sunday AM breakfast/check-out/departure.

The timetable is programme-specific: do not advertise an exam simply because it is Semester 2. Exams are enabled only where the relevant programme/awarding-body assessment plan requires/allows them.

## Attendance
Centre/gate access proves building presence but not class attendance. Each required activity has separate attendance. Placement hosts verify work-experience attendance. Exam attendance follows the exam-control workflow. Residential completion aggregates required activity attendance without overwriting raw access records.

## Included residential operations
Accommodation, meals, welfare/emergency information, rooming, trips/events where scheduled, centre access pass, individual timetable, placements, expo appointments and departure workflow.
