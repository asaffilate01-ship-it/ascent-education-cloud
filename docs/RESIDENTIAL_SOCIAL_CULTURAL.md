# Residential Social, Cultural & Sightseeing Programme

Each applicable learner has two Islamabad residential academic weeks per academic year, one per semester. Social/cultural activities complement rather than displace required academic contact, assessment or placement hours.

## Programme design
Semester 1 can carry the stronger welcome/community component: welcome dinner, cohort networking, staff/lecturer introductions, Islamabad cultural/sightseeing activity and closing social event.
Semester 2 remains assessment/progression-led but can include a lighter cultural/social programme and closing dinner.

Potential Islamabad/nearby activities are configured by operations rather than hard-coded. Examples may include Faisal Mosque, Pakistan Monument/Museum, Lok Virsa, Saidpur Village, Rawal Lake/viewpoints or a separately risk-assessed Taxila excursion. Availability, suitability, transport, opening arrangements and safeguarding must be checked for each actual trip.

## Trip control
Every excursion records destination, times, capacity, transport plan, staff lead, risk assessment, emergency notes and whether included or optional/charged. Participant tracking supports registration/consent and four operational checkpoints: boarded outward, arrived, boarded return, returned.

## Safeguarding
Where consent is legally/policy required, participation stays pending until consent is recorded. Stronger rules apply to minors/tuition learners. Do not use the building gate record as proof that a learner boarded/returned from a trip.

## Commercial model
Core welcome/social/cultural activities can be included within the residential-week package. Optional premium excursions may carry a disclosed additional fee. Academic attendance requirements must never depend on buying an optional trip.
