- blind/pseudonymous marker reference where operationally appropriate
- workload/SLA balancing and conflict declaration
- AI criterion draft + human criterion decision

- resubmission V1/V2 comparison
- IQA sample/action/release

## Phase B — Integrity operations
- Turnitin connector adapter and webhook/event storage

- KYC/NADRA connector, certificates, education, employment and references

## Phase E — physical centre
