## Competitive opportunity

Most visible competitor marketing leads with studying or obtaining a visa **abroad**. UniPathway can differentiate credibly through **what it teaches and trains in Pakistan** before university progression:
1. **Tuition**: Years 8–12 science, maths, English and technology; online and in-centre; Matric/FSc/Cambridge.
2. **Qualifications and teaching**: UK/international diploma planning, verified specifications, online LMS, supervised academic weeks where authorised.
3. **Career skills**: AI, Python, cyber skills, cloud, finance, IFA and ACCA tutoring, vocational subject portfolios and eligible programmes.
4. **Languages**: IELTS English plus German A1–C1 and exam guidance; proper entry requirements for each European destination.
5. **Educational progression**: Pakistan universities and global university research; actual receiving institution determines qualification equivalence.
6. **Support**: Accommodation, finances, employment rights, career planning and properly regulated immigration referral.
7. **Community**: Ilmvero school partnerships, independently verified employer openings, merit-and-need scholarship development with confidentiality.

## Marketing guidance
Prioritise course demos, parent/student learning concerns and real student work over repetitive visa promotions. Suggest 3–4 posts weekly, mix a short video Q&A with a text/card post and a programme explainer, and respond privately to questions involving grades, personal records or financial need.

**Always avoid:** fake approvals, fabricated student success numbers, misleading "no IELTS required everywhere", guaranteed university admission, visa promises, guaranteed free places, or unverified sponsoring employers. Advertising a European country is not a university partnership.
