# Regional Student Life in the LMS

All student-life discovery, registration, teams, fixtures, payments, notices, safeguarding information and results live inside the LMS/app.

## Geographic model
Students have an operational student-life city/region (not a lecturer-visible home address). Initial hubs can include Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad/Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan, Peshawar and additional cities as numbers justify.

Students see activities relevant to:
1. their city/local hub
2. their School/Department
3. centre-wide/national activities
4. external/inter-university opportunities

## Competition ladder
Local/city events can qualify teams or individuals into regional/provincial events, then national finals. Parent/child competition records preserve the ladder.

Example:
Karachi football -> Sindh regional -> UniPathway Pakistan national final.
Lahore debate -> Punjab regional -> national debate championship.
City chess/coding/business-case events can follow the same model.

## Cost sharing
Activities may be free, subsidised or carry a transparent participant subscription/entry fee in PKR to contribute to venue, officials, equipment, kit, transport, refreshments or event costs. Fee, inclusions, refund/waiver rules and any subsidy are shown before registration. Finance records payment/waiver/refund.

Keep student-life fees separate from tuition and do not make optional paid activities a condition of academic progression.

## Operations
Each city activity hub can have a coordinator, venue, capacity and event calendar. LMS handles registration, team formation, waiting lists, fixtures, announcements, results and digital recognition.

## Safety
For physical events collect only operationally necessary information, publish emergency contacts and venue instructions, use authorised coordinators, risk assessments and incident workflows, and keep private addresses hidden from lecturers/other students.
