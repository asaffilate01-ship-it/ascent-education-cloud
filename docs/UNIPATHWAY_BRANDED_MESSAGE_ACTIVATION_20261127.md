# Branded admissions and recruitment messages

Public visitors may use their own personal email address; this is not a login or account creation. Admissions enquiries retain the existing CRM lead and go to admissions@unipathway.pk. Teaching expressions of interest go to jobs@unipathway.pk and a private recruitment intake record, never the student lead pipeline. All teaching arrangements are expressions of interest for part-time online, hybrid or in-class work. No role, appointment, job offer or admission is granted.

Both routes send from UniPathway <notifications@unipathway.pk> using the Resend Email API. The team message includes the submitted details and reply-to address; the applicant confirmation is generic with a reference and does not reflect user-supplied content. Provider acceptance is not guaranteed inbox delivery. No external recipient can be supplied as the internal team mailbox.

## Recovery and retention

Submission and email provider acceptance are distinct. A partial or uncertain response retains the request and locks the original details in the open page. A fresh CAPTCHA can retry the identical command; the database stores exactly one intake and lead where applicable. The service uses fixed recipient/channel idempotency keys and durable provider IDs, with a conservative 23-hour resend window (Resend retains idempotency keys for 24 hours). Outside that window, or after leaving/reloading the page, contact the relevant team with the reference before resubmitting. An authorised operator must reconcile pending delivery records and provider IDs. There is no scheduled retry worker or delivery webhook in this release. Approve retention, deletion, bounce handling, restricted HR access, and an operator reconciliation process before activation.

There is a durable per-email hourly quota and an initial global 20-new-request/hour budget across the new route, plus CAPTCHA and an in-process IP limit. Review actual load and abuse patterns before changing limits. No CVs, identity documents, bank details, passwords, or student records are requested. A real mailbox, tested credentials, data protection approval and live delivery evidence remain required. No production configuration, mail or database writes occurred during this repository change.
