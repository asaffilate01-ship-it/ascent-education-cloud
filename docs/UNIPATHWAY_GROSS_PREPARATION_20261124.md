# Closed-month teaching remuneration evidence

Depends on the timesheet and rate-agreement activation candidates. Only a verified assigned finance officer can prepare a completed Pakistan teaching month, and a separate verified centre director can review or reject that exact snapshot. No pending claims, missing/overlapping rates or unresolved partial flat-rate sessions may be included in a prepared snapshot. Neither preparer nor reviewer may have their own approved claim in the snapshot. Source IDs, rate IDs, review references, dates, minutes and grouped gross amounts are stored in a private immutable-source record. The server computes the fingerprint and all amounts; the browser checks receipts and reconciliation. Exact retries return the recorded result.

A late or changed claim can make a reviewed snapshot stale. Each new read compares the full source with the stored snapshot; acceptance also rechecks it. The workflow is an evidence review as of the recorded review, not a lock on future timesheets, a completed payroll or payment approval. A rejected preparation can be replaced; a reviewed preparation cannot be replaced in this version. Correction/supplementary batches, contract verification, deductions, tax, payroll authorisation, payment and payslips are separate future work. Do not treat absence of pending records as proof that all work has been reported.

Activation: review and run the exact forward SQL in a controlled transaction only after the timesheet and rate schema are present and accepted in the correct project. Gates default off; frontend independently requires VITE_UNIPATHWAY_GROSS_PREPARATION_ENABLED=true. Test backup/restore, tenant and role isolation, disabled gates, current/stale snapshots, independent review, reconciliation and real user journeys. The synthetic CI rehearsal is not hosted activation. Limit is 500 source claims per month; larger centres require paginated reporting before adoption. No production mutation or payment is performed by this release.
