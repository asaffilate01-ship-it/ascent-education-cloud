# UniPathway curriculum pilot: review before release

This checklist is for staff. It is not a statement of current accreditation, compliance, publication or learner access. The generated review inventory tracks authored items only and is not an LMS import file.

| Gate | Minimum evidence | Stop condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Subject review | Named educator independently runs the key or lab and records corrections against item ID and version. | Unsafe task, wrong solution, unlicensed input or insufficient instructions. |
| Route mapping | Record the exact cohort, authority, syllabus/document URL and edition, component, outcome and fit (full, partial, excluded or enrichment). | Unknown board, component, awarding-body approval scope or exam session. Keep generic practice separate. |
| Accessibility and delivery | Verify readable mobile presentation, alternative formats where needed, offline or low-bandwidth instructions, and clearly separated teacher answer material. | The lesson cannot be delivered with the centre's equipment, support or supervision. |
| Pilot placement | Confirm the intended module and cohort; start with a small set selected and approved by the programme lead. Keep an item ID and revision to destination lesson ID map. | Course, target cohort, permissions or publication state cannot be verified. No write without a confirmed destination. |
| Main-site learner check | Verify normal access from the intended learner account and no visibility for unrelated learners or unauthenticated visitors where the lesson is cohort-only. Confirm that tutors can see the correct version and answer resources. | Role check fails, source revision disagrees, or expected content does not render. Remove the assignment or unpublish through the normal workflow; retain the review record. |

## Counting and versioning

Record one canonical ID per distinct teaching objective, independent task and evidence of learning. A second board mapping, translated version, alternate input data or answer sheet does not count as another learning item. Before reporting distinct progress, reconcile potential overlap across waves and confirm the selected route; do not add incompatible coverage counts. Revisions keep their ID and add a review record for the new version.

The inventory is generated from teaching files, never edited to mark an approval. To store a review, separately record item ID, content revision, reviewer and date, source/version, scope, decision and notes. Store a release record only after confirming destination ID, assigned cohort, release revision, access verification and rollback owner. No real learner names or account details belong in Git.

## Practical review questions

- Punjab, Sindh, Federal and Cambridge: what exact grade, entry or qualification and exam window is the learner taking? Is this a theory, practical or enrichment activity?
- ACCA: what sitting, paper and variant apply? Do not use a new qualification label to imply transition credit.
- Business, accounting, health and social care, and tech: what specific awarding body, qualification and unit is approved at the centre? Is the evidence formative only?
- IELTS and languages: is the activity for Academic, General Training, Life Skills or general communication? Check provider material separately if exam alignment is needed; do not infer visa acceptance.
- Tech labs: can another instructor reproduce the expected result with the stated inputs and tools offline? Use synthetic data and provide a reset instruction or fresh copy.
