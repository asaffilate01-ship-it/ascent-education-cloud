# Wave 2: IELTS and language skills

**Status:** Original staff-review material. These are short formative activities, not complete exam tasks, scored mocks, official materials, or evidence of immigration eligibility. [IELTS test types](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types) distinguishes Academic and General Training from [IELTS for UKVI Life Skills](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-tests-for-uk-visas-and-immigration/ielts-life-skills-test/life-skills-test-format-in-detail), which assesses speaking and listening. Level labels below are teaching targets, not route or local availability advice. Use fictional names and contact details; do not record learners by default. For oral work, give learners their role cards only and withhold sample replies and answer keys until feedback.

## W2-LAN-01 · Explain the sequence in a process diagram

**Audience:** IELTS Academic Writing Task 1 skill practice.

**Objective:** Give an overview and distinguish two steps with clear reference words.

**Worked example:** Library returns → desk staff scan books → staff sort books by floor → books go back onto shelves. “Overall, the process takes books from the returns point back to the shelves. Staff first scan each book and then sort the returns by the floor on which they belong.”

**Practice:** Community notices → volunteer checks event date → coordinator approves text → notice published online. Write an overview and a second sentence describing the first two checks. Do not invent a time or reason.

**Key:** “Overall, notices are checked and approved before they are published online. A volunteer first checks each event date, and the coordinator then approves the text.” Check sequence, roles and lack of invented details. This short response is not a full exam answer.

## W2-LAN-02 · State what happened and what you want done

**Audience:** IELTS General Training Writing Task 1 skill practice.

**Objective:** Write a factual complaint and a proportionate request without sounding hostile.

**Worked example:** “The timetable advertised a 9 a.m. session, but the room was locked when I arrived. Could you please confirm the next session and transfer my booking to it?”

**Practice:** A fictional community hall let you book a table and four chairs; two chairs were provided. In three sentences, state your booking, the problem and one precise request for next Saturday.

**Key:** “I booked a table and four chairs, but only two chairs were provided. I have another booking next Saturday. Could you please confirm that the full set of four chairs will be available then?” Other solutions work if they are polite, specific and do not add facts.

## W2-LAN-03 · Hear one instruction and check the meeting point

**Audience:** IELTS for UKVI Life Skills A1 speaking/listening practice.

**Objective:** Carry out a simple spoken instruction and confirm two details with a partner.

**Worked example:** Tutor says, “Please tell your partner to meet you at the bakery at five.” Learner: “Can we meet at the bakery at five?” Partner: “Yes. At the bakery at five.”

**Practice:** Tutor privately tells learner A, “Tell your partner to meet you by the park gate at three.” A relays it; B asks one confirmation question; A responds. Swap roles with a new place and time. Do not show the tutor's words to the learners beforehand.

**Rubric:** One point each for an accurate place, accurate time and relevant confirmation interaction. Sample: “Meet me by the park gate at three, please.” “At the park gate?” “Yes, at three.” Practice result only.

## W2-LAN-04 · Discuss a change and reach agreement

**Audience:** IELTS for UKVI Life Skills B1 speaking/listening practice.

**Objective:** Respond to a partner’s new information, give a reason and agree on a practical change.

**Worked example:** A: “The outdoor event might be cancelled because of strong winds.” B: “In that case, we could meet in the café. It's open later than the library.” A: “Good idea. Let's meet there at six.”

**Practice:** A knows a bus trip must change because the usual stop is closed. Read that fact aloud to B. B has two options: walk eight minutes to another stop, or get a later bus from outside the library. Compare the options and agree on a meeting place and time; finish by restating the agreed plan.

**Rubric:** One point each for acknowledging the closure, comparing two options with a reason, and stating a mutually agreed plan. Sample: “Let’s use the stop eight minutes away so we don’t miss the film.” “Agreed. I’ll meet you outside the library at 5:10 and we’ll walk together.” No official result is implied.

## W2-LAN-05 · Make a request and respond

**Audience:** German A1 speaking practice; builds on LAN-12.

**Objective:** Request a simple action politely and respond to a partner in the same register.

**Worked example:** A: „Können Sie bitte die Tür schließen?“ B: „Ja, natürlich.“ Or: „Tut mir leid. Vielleicht später?“

**Practice:** Give A a lit-lamp picture and ask them to request that the lamp be turned off. Give B a closed-window picture and ask them to request that the window be opened. Partners reply to each request before changing roles.

**Rubric:** One point each for a comprehensible request, a relevant reply and polite language. Sample: „Können Sie bitte die Lampe ausmachen?“ „Ja, natürlich.“ „Können Sie bitte das Fenster öffnen?“ „Tut mir leid. Es ist kalt. Vielleicht später?“ Do not require these exact words. See [Goethe adult A1 speaking practice](https://bfu.goethe.de/a1_sd1/sprechen.php) for the task family, not a complete test replica.

## W2-LAN-06 · Ask which document to bring

**Audience:** German everyday beginner.

**Objective:** Ask what is needed for a fictional service appointment and check your understanding.

**Worked example:** „Was soll ich zum Termin mitbringen?“ „Bitte bringen Sie den Terminbrief mit.“ „Also den Terminbrief, richtig?“ The separated verb *mitbringen* ends the question and becomes *bringen … mit* in the instruction.

**Practice:** At a fictional sports club, ask what to bring for a trial session. The clerk says, „Bitte bringen Sie Ihre Sportschuhe mit.“ Repeat the item as a confirmation question.

**Key:** „Was soll ich zum Probetraining mitbringen?“ „Also meine Sportschuhe, richtig?“ Accept a natural equivalent with the correct item. This fictional club request is not a visa-document checklist.

## W2-LAN-07 · Complete a form and write a short invitation

**Audience:** French beginner writing; optional DELF A1 Tout Public format awareness.

**Objective:** Transfer four details to a form and write a separate friendly note of at least 40 words.

**Worked example:** A fictional profile “Maya Noor, maya@example.test, jeudi à 14 h” becomes “Prénom : Maya ; nom : Noor ; courriel : maya@example.test ; séance : jeudi à 14 h.” A friendly note can open “Salut Zoé,” and close “À bientôt !”.

**Practice:** Given Lina Ahmed, lina@example.test, cooking workshop Saturday at 10:00, complete the four fields. In a separate note of at least 40 words to Camille, explain why you want to go, tell Camille when and where to meet, and ask for a reply.

**Rubric:** One point per accurate field. Mark the note separately: four points for reason, day/time, meeting place and reply request; up to two for comprehensibility; one for friendly register; one for at least 40 words. Model: “Bonjour Camille, samedi je vais à un atelier de cuisine avec ma sœur. Je veux apprendre à faire un gâteau. Est-ce que tu veux venir avec nous ? On se retrouve à dix heures devant la bibliothèque. Tu peux me répondre avant vendredi ? À bientôt, Lina.” Count apostrophised and hyphenated forms as one word and ignore detached punctuation. See [France Éducation international DELF A1 format](https://www.france-education-international.fr/diplome/delf-tout-public/niveau-a1?langue=en). This is not an official marking scale.

## W2-LAN-08 · Say what you missed and ask for a simple recap

**Audience:** French everyday beginner.

**Objective:** Explain why you missed a session and ask for the next step in polite French.

**Worked example:** « Je n’ai pas pu venir lundi. Est-ce que vous pouvez me dire ce que je dois lire ? » Use *je n’ai pas pu* to report being unable to do something.

**Practice:** You missed Wednesday’s cooking class. Tell the tutor when you missed it and ask what equipment to bring next time. Tutor replies « Apportez un tablier. » Confirm the item.

**Key:** « Je n’ai pas pu venir mercredi. Qu’est-ce que je dois apporter la prochaine fois ? Un tablier, c’est bien ça ? » Check past day, future action and *tablier*.

## W2-LAN-09 · Find groceries by quantity and unit

**Audience:** Italian everyday beginner.

**Objective:** Ask for a quantity and recognise the difference between half a kilo and one kilo.

**Worked example:** « Vorrei mezzo chilo di mele, per favore. » The vendor asks « Mezzo chilo o un chilo? » A simple clarification is « Mezzo chilo, grazie. »

**Practice:** Ask for one kilo of oranges and two lemons. The vendor intentionally repeats “half a kilo of oranges” and “two lemons”; correct only the quantity that is wrong.

**Key:** « Vorrei un chilo di arance e due limoni, per favore. » Correction: « Un chilo di arance, non mezzo chilo. E due limoni, grazie. »

## W2-LAN-10 · Report a missed delivery in a short message

**Audience:** Italian everyday beginner writing.

**Objective:** Explain that nobody was home and request a new delivery slot.

**Worked example:** « Non ero a casa questa mattina. È possibile consegnare il pacco domani? » *Ero* describes the past situation.

**Practice:** Write two sentences to a fictional courier. Nobody was home yesterday afternoon; ask if delivery on Friday morning is possible. Do not include a real address or tracking number.

**Key:** « Ieri pomeriggio non c’era nessuno in casa. È possibile consegnare il pacco venerdì mattina? » Accept another friendly polite formulation with the same details.

## W2-LAN-11 · Ask how to borrow a book

**Audience:** Spanish everyday beginner.

**Objective:** Ask about borrowing rather than buying and repeat a return deadline.

**Worked example:** « ¿Puedo llevarme este libro prestado? » « Sí. Devuélvalo antes del jueves. » « De acuerdo, antes del jueves. »

**Practice:** At a fictional library, ask whether you can borrow two books. The librarian says « Sí, hasta el lunes. » Confirm the return day.

**Key:** « ¿Puedo llevarme estos dos libros prestados? » « Entonces, ¿los devuelvo el lunes? » Accept « El lunes, ¿verdad? » for the confirmation. Check plural *libros* and the return day.

## W2-LAN-12 · Compare two community classes

**Audience:** Spanish beginner bridge speaking.

**Objective:** Offer two alternatives, explain a preference and agree on one.

**Worked example:** « El curso de dibujo es el martes, pero el de cocina es el sábado. Prefiero el de cocina porque trabajo el martes. » The partner can accept or suggest a different option.

**Practice:** There is a Wednesday swim class and a Saturday dance class. A cannot attend Wednesday; B prefers swimming but can attend both days. Say your constraints and agree on a class to try together.

**Rubric:** One point each for two identifiable options, a reason or schedule constraint, and a shared spoken decision. Sample: « Prefiero nadar, pero tú trabajas el miércoles. Podemos probar el baile el sábado. » « De acuerdo, vamos a la clase de baile. »
