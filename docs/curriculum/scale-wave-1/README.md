# UniPathway | Curriculum expansion, wave 1

**110 original staff-review micro-lessons and labs**, not 3,200 published lessons. Each short teaching item includes a worked example, an independent task and an answer or observable success criteria. The teacher can use these as the core of a session after local review; they are not complete taught-hour plans, official exam papers or formal assessments.

| Pack | Items | Coverage in this wave |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| [School and Cambridge](school.md) | 23 | Four per Pakistan Grade 8–12, plus three Cambridge examples for Lower Secondary, IGCSE Mathematics and AS Physics |
| [ACCA](acca.md) | 23 | Foundations, Applied Knowledge, all Applied Skills papers, Strategic Professional Essentials and Options, ethics, plus the three current CAT specialist options |
| [Vocational](vocational.md) | 24 | All 20 combinations of Levels 2–6 and Business, Accounting, Health and Social Care, and Technology; four additional applications |
| [Languages and IELTS](languages.md) | 24 | Eight IELTS skill drills and four each for German, French, Italian and Spanish |
| [Technology labs](labs.md) | 16 | Ten Grade 8–12 labs and six across Levels 2–6, using local or paper tools and simulated information |

[Coverage register and review rules](coverage-register.md) sets the 3,200-item target and separates planning, writing, review, mapping and release. It includes two partial Cambridge outcome mappings; the remaining entries need route-specific review. Use unique IDs for reviews and count reuse only once. This wave is additive to the [earlier 68-item pack](../next-teaching-batch/README.md) and does not depend on the separate school extension PR #239. No LMS import, approval or student publication is implied.
