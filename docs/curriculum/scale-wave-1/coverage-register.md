## Route identification and sequencing

- **School:** First map Grade 8 by school curriculum and Grades 9–12 by administering board, SSC/HSSC year, group, code, medium and exam session. Cover Maths, English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computing first, then applicable Urdu, Pakistan Studies, general science, commerce and other subjects once each scope is pinned. Treat theory, practicals and coursework as separate components. Start with the cohort the centre actually teaches; do not extrapolate one provincial model paper to another board.
- **Cambridge:** Identify programme stage, syllabus code, applicable years, paper or component, practical route and scope. The Cambridge stage is not a claim of equivalence to a Pakistan grade.
- **ACCA:** Identify qualification or paper, official syllabus version, applicable exam window, variant where relevant, outcome and exam task. Keep Foundations-level awards, the ACCA Qualification, official ethics modules and practical experience requirements separate; tutoring does not confer exemption, credit or completion.
- **Vocational:** Verify the awarding body, qualification number, approved centre scope, unit reference, actual learning outcome, assessment criteria and permitted evidence. These internal Level 2–6 difficulty guides are not an accreditation map. Final assessment and IQA are separate from lesson drafting.
- **Languages:** Identify test type, skill and task; keep Academic and General Training variants separate when the real task differs. For other languages identify level, communicative function and provider if targeted. General language tuition is not an approved visa test or a promise of acceptance.
- **Labs:** Assign the actual tool and version, accessibility support, intended learner group and supervision. Use fictional inputs and safe offline work; instructor reviews classroom suitability and expected output before release.

## Entry and review rules

Each item has one stable ID, unique title, audience, objective, worked example, learner task and answer or observable success criteria. The first wave is formative and ready for **staff review**, not ready for instruction or official decisions without review. One canonical lesson can map to several verified outcomes; count it once. Create a new ID only if the teaching objective, task and evidence of learning are materially different. Translation, a different dataset, a new filename and answer sheets do not raise the count.

Record for each mapping: stable item ID; route; authority; document URL and version; code and component; outcome reference and plain-language paraphrase; exam window/group/medium; fit (full, partial, enrichment, or excluded); reviewer and date. Preserve exclusion notices and their exact applicability. Before release, require subject review, checked solution, copyright/source review, appropriate safety and accessibility, and a confirmed destination in the correct course and cohort. To call a syllabus fully covered, reconcile every required outcome and assessment component for a specific entry, not just the item count.

Track release stages per item: **planned → drafted → subject-reviewed → route-mapped → approved → published and verified**. Drafted means complete enough for a reviewer to use. Mapping and approvals require actual recordable evidence. Keep published totals tied to verified learner access, not GitHub merge status.
