# UniPathway Grade 8–12 tuition: coverage and release matrix

**Planning baseline, sources checked 20 November 2026.** Mathematics, English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computer Science are six content families, not six compulsory papers. Topics below are authoring seeds, not certified grade assignments, coverage already written, or a complete curriculum. Confirm the exact grade/stage, subject variant, outcomes and exam session before labelling or selling a board-specific course.

Grade 8 Physics, Chemistry and Biology appear as **General Science strands** for local paths and Science strands on a Cambridge Lower Secondary path, not three independent exam tracks. Grade 9–12 subject eligibility and compulsory/elective combinations depend on the chosen board and group; capture the group and variant before offering a bundle. Generic mathematics and English outcomes are not automatically the science-group or compulsory-English paper.

**Equivalence:** SSC refers to local Parts I/II in IX/X and HSSC/Intermediate to Parts I/II in XI/XII in the local planning matrix. Formal foreign qualification equivalence is a separate [IBCC determination](https://ibcc.edu.pk/equivalence-intro/british-system/) subject to examination location, subject passes, qualification and study group. The six tuition families do not cover all IBCC conditions or all local compulsory subjects. UniPathway tutoring is not Cambridge affiliation, exam entry, school credit or a qualification.

## Authoring and release gates

1. **Common core pilot:** propose 30 planning cells (five bands × six families); for Grade 8 three science cells share the parent General Science course. Use the prior batch and this extension as the opening inventory: each of the 30 cells has at least two targeted lesson drafts. For each further lesson include a stated objective, diagnostic, worked example, differentiated practice, worked answers and teacher check. Share concept content only where accurate; keep official examples, paper format, vocabulary, sequencing and required practicals in explicit overlays. Default to “foundation practice” unless mapped.
2. **Board pilot:** start with one named board/session and one verified unit per selected subject. Manifest key: `route | board | qualification | grade/stage/part | group | subject/code | medium | academic year | exam session | source URL+edition | outcome ID | component | asset IDs | status | reviewer/date`. Preserve no-map reasons and session expiry. Evidence of syllabus mapping does not prove lessons are authored.
3. **Track release:** map all chosen syllabus outcomes and components, including English formats, Mathematics methods, Science practical/data skills and CS language/pseudocode requirements. Audit active vs exam-excluded outcomes, independently verify worked answers/mark allocations, test mobile rendering and navigation, and have a teacher sign off. Virtual labs are practical preparation, not lab completion. Use only original material or properly licensed extracts; link official past papers instead of republishing.
4. **Display truthful availability:** status lifecycle `planned → source-pinned → mapped → authored → teacher-reviewed → published`. Show exact scope (e.g. “3 reviewed units available”), not “full board prep,” until all selected outcomes and components are independently reviewed and published. Surface “not yet available” for gaps and route uncertain learners to a diagnostic or adviser, not a guessed equivalence.

Outstanding before a board-specific claim: signed-off outcome counts and markers, Urdu-medium variants and bilingual terminology QA, practical evidence/lab provision, Karachi 2027 board references, Cambridge exam-year/zone/code eligibility, and the actual lesson inventory and publish state. This planning document does not attest to any of those being complete.
