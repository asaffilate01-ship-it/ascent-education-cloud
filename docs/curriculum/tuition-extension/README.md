# UniPathway tuition extension: Grades 8–12

**20 November 2026 · Staff review only.** This adds **30 grade-targeted lessons** across Maths, English, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Computing, plus **six cross-grade progress-check workshops**. Each grade has one new lesson per subject. For Grade 8, the three science lessons are strands of General Science, not offers of three separate examination subjects.

Together with the school content from [the previous batch](../next-teaching-batch/pakistan-school-subjects.md) and [its six school follow-ons](../next-teaching-batch/follow-on-workshops.md#school-subjects), this gives **66 grade-targeted school lessons plus six review workshops**. Accountancy, ACCA, business, social care, IELTS and languages remain in their separate existing packs and are not counted here.

## Which work remains before full tuition coverage?

1. **Pin entries.** Confirm the school’s actual board, grade/part, group, subject code, medium, textbook/version and examination year. For Cambridge, use the exact qualification/code/year/component; do not convert local grades into Cambridge stages.
2. **Expand the lesson bank against those entries.** Create a board-specific outcome inventory including every assessed component, mark what already has usable material, and commission missing sequences, retrieval sets and timed original mocks. Confirm English literature/set texts and compulsory/elective papers separately. Plan bilingual terminology and accessible media where needed.
3. **Close the practical gap.** Teachers must match approved practical requirements, equipment, supervision and safety; supplied datasets and diagrams are not completion of practical work. Use externally available official papers by link where rights do not allow copying.
4. **Review and release.** A subject educator checks every key and placement, then a named programme lead signs off the complete outcome and assessment coverage before any “full course” wording. Publish to the correct approved learner course only after confirming access. Nothing in this document uploads lessons or changes production.

**First exam-prep pilot:** Start with a named Punjab Grade 9 entry and exam year rather than a generic board toggle. In Punjab’s Annual BISE 2027 Computer Science & Entrepreneurship route, use the original decision and flowchart practice in this pack. Treat the earlier CS09 binary lesson as optional enrichment for that exam; the topics are excluded under PECTAA notification 1255 (14 November 2026), Annex A, Unit 1. This is a cohort-specific exception, not a removal from other verified routes. Match the model-paper format separately for any Matric-Tech entry. [Official notification](https://pectaa.edu.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ALP-Notification-14.11.2026-1255-1.pdf).
