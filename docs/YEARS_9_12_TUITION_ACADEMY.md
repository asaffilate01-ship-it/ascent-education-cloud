# Years 9–12 Tuition Academy

## Positioning
A separate school-support/coaching product inside UniPathway, distinct from regulated higher/pathway qualifications. Students may buy one subject or a package.

## Initial subjects
Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, English, Computer Science / IT / Technology. Biology can be added as a natural next subject where demand warrants.

## Curriculum routes
Do not assume one Pakistan syllabus. Track curriculum/exam-board/version explicitly. Initial support can include FBISE and selected provincial BISE routes, followed by Cambridge O Level/A Level. Pakistan's curriculum is devolved to provinces after the 18th Amendment, while NCC sets national standards/frameworks; therefore content must be board/version mapped rather than labelled generically "Pakistan Grade 9". 

## Product
- live online small-group classes
- centre classes where viable
- recorded revision library
- chapter/topic diagnostics
- homework help and worked examples
- weekly quizzes
- past-paper style practice where licensing/copyright permits
- timed mock exams
- AI study assistant constrained to the enrolled syllabus
- teacher office hours
- progress analytics and weak-topic recommendations
- parent dashboard for attendance, mock results and progress
- exam countdown/revision planner

## Safeguarding
Years 9–12 may include minors. Staff communication remains LMS/institutional channels only; no private WhatsApp/personal phone/personal email. Guardian linkage, safeguarding escalation and age-appropriate data access apply.

## Scheduling
Reuse the verified teacher pool, module/subject competency and availability engine. Only teachers approved for the specific subject/board/grade can be scheduled.

## Commercial options
Per-subject monthly subscription; multi-subject bundle; intensive revision/mock package; centre premium; employer/community scholarship/voucher. Keep pricing configurable in PKR.
