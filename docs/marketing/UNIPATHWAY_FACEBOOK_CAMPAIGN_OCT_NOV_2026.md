# UniPathway Facebook launch campaign — October–November 2026

## Research and creative positioning
**Brand proposition:** *We teach. We train. We guide. We help you progress.*  
**Primary tagline:** *Learn here. Go further.*  
**Role:** A teaching, training, qualifications and learner-support organisation operating from Pakistan; overseas admissions are complementary to education delivery.  
**Palette:** Deep Navy `#0B2D5B` · Fresh Green `#16A34A` · White `#FFFFFF` · Light Neutral `#F3F6FA`. Use the selected approved UniPathway logo, not a recreated UP glyph.

**Competitor observation:** Publicly indexed Ignite Consultants Facebook content shows a recurring student-character storyline (“Hassan”), IELTS myth explainer, and Germany affordability messages; Times Consultant frequently presents destination guides, visa outcomes and student stories; IDP promotes education fairs and expert IELTS masterclasses. For UniPathway, keep the story-led engagement but lead with teaching and practical skills, not student-visa statistics, guaranteed outcomes or invented success stories.

**Copy compliance:** Planned qualifications and programme modes must not be labelled currently accredited or open until approved. Do not use the CIBTAC, CPD UK, OTHM, Qualifi or British Council marks without appropriate permission. Do not claim active university relationships or guaranteed UK visa/graduate routes. Obtain student/guardian consent before publishing personal photographs, exam results or testimonials. Application forms must never request sensitive family financial records through ordinary Facebook messages.

**Call-to-action:** Visit **unipathway.pk** (subject to site publication) or message the UniPathway Facebook page for current course dates and eligibility.  
**Posting suggestion:** Publish 3–4 organic posts per week; alternate parent/student education, skills demonstrations, polls and transparent progression content. The dates below are proposed, not scheduled.

## Campaign hygiene

- Use approved navy/green brand and correct UniPathway logo throughout, with visual hierarchy that works on mobile.
- Suggested portrait canvas: 1080 × 1350 px, and alternate 1080 × 1080 px for simple carousel cover. These are creative briefs, not generated images.
- Publish course dates and admissions forms only after relevant public pages, enquiry security and provider approvals are functioning.
- Keep contact CTAs in-platform and at `unipathway.pk`; do not invent phone numbers, live cohorts, pass rates, campus approvals or visa outcomes.
- If running genuine student stories, obtain explicit photo/testimonial permissions and guardian sign-off when needed. Do not use fabricated admissions success figures.
- Treat EU destinations and university examples as informational research until signed institutional relationships and progression evidence are confirmed.
- Check every post against the live operational readiness tracker before scheduling.
