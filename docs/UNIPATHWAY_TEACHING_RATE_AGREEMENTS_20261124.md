# Teaching rate register and remuneration preparation

Part-time teaching remuneration uses actual independently approved teaching claims from the timesheet service. A verified centre director proposes a finite, inclusive effective-date range, PKR hourly or full-session rate and reference to an externally agreed teaching arrangement. A separate assigned finance officer approves or rejects the proposal. Self-approval, overlapping pending/approved agreements and mutable approval records are prevented through scoped server operations. The rate does not establish legal employment status or generate a signed contract.

The finance-only monthly preview matches approved claims to the approved rate in force on each teaching date. Hourly calculations round once per lecturer/rate group to paisa. Full-session rates apply only to approved claims with actual minutes equal to planned minutes; partial flat-rate sessions and missing rates are explicitly unresolved, with no calculated amount. Pending teaching claims are excluded and counted, not paid. The preview is not a frozen payroll batch or payment authority. Rate correction, withdrawal, supplementary claims, deductions, taxes, expenses, payroll approval and transfers require separate verified workflows.

Install `scripts/activation/unipathway-teaching-rates-forward.sql` only after the preceding timesheet schema and gates have passed actual centre acceptance. Review backup/recovery, schema and access grants; run the synthetic CI rehearsal; verify real role JWTs and data isolation. The new read/write gates are disabled by default, and the frontend additionally requires `VITE_UNIPATHWAY_TEACHING_RATES_ENABLED=true`. Do not enable writes before authenticated acceptance. No hosted SQL executed by this release. Finance review notes and agreement references are scoped to the authorised director and finance roles, not general office/HR access.
