# Unified Education Platform

UniPathway runs one shared identity/LMS/finance/careers platform with separate academic engines.

- Regulated pathways: OTHM/Qualifi/IAB and future approved provision use controlled specification, specialist marking, IQA/EQA and regulated release.
- Pakistan skills: NAVTTC/NVQF/QAB provision uses competency/practical evidence and approved assessment/certification.
- Tuition Academy: Years 9-12 board/version mapped teaching, homework, quizzes, mocks, parent progress and safeguarding.
- Employer Academy: short courses and custom corporate cohorts, company-paid/employee-paid/subsidised delivery and HR completion reporting.
- UniPathway short courses: accurately labelled internal/endorsed provision, never represented as externally regulated without recorded approval.

Every course has an academic_engine, certification label, regulator/awarder, approval state, delivery modes, practical/residential settings, funding settings and cohort-specific payment model.

Shared services: KYC/NADRA, education verification, admissions, payments/funding, attendance, scheduling, messaging, safeguarding, student life, careers, internships/jobs, progression, partner network and analytics.
