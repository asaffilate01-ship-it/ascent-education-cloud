# Careers, Internships & Global Progression

## Student LMS areas
### Internships
Verified employers publish internships. Students discover/filter, review requirements, apply using their verified UniPathway profile/CV, explicitly consent to data sharing, track status, interviews and offers.

### Jobs
Pakistan and international vacancies share the same marketplace. Filters include location, remote, sector, graduate role and sponsorship availability. Employer/recruiter accounts must be verified before publication.

### Sponsorship connector
International jobs may be checked through a provider-neutral Sponsor SaaS connector/API. Store the check date, country, provider reference and returned sponsorship evidence. Never state that a role guarantees a visa; sponsorship capability and immigration eligibility remain separate questions.

### Universities Abroad
A full case workspace from planning through arrival:
DISCOVERY -> ELIGIBILITY -> SHORTLIST -> DOCUMENTS -> APPLICATION -> OFFER -> CONDITIONS -> DEPOSIT -> ACCOMMODATION -> PRE-DEPARTURE -> ARRIVAL -> ACCLIMATISATION -> ONGOING SUPPORT.

Countries can include UK, USA, Australia, Germany and additional approved destinations.

Counsellor dashboard manages tasks, documents, deadlines, offers and partner communications. Student sees one timeline/checklist in the LMS.

## Accommodation & arrival
Support can include accommodation search/application, pre-departure briefing, airport/arrival guidance, local registration/signposting, banking/SIM/transport orientation, university induction, wellbeing contacts and acclimatisation check-ins. Where immigration advice is regulated, refer to appropriately authorised advisers.

## University partner commission
Record expected commission only from signed partner terms. Track qualifying event, student consent/referral, enrolment confirmation, invoice, paid/declined/reconciled. Commission must not influence the academic suitability advice presented to the student.

## Employer portal
Verified companies can create internships/jobs, manage applicants, shortlist/interview and update outcomes. They see only student data explicitly shared for an application.

## AI
- Opportunity Matching Agent: recommends internships/jobs from verified skills, course, location and preferences.
- CV/Cover Letter Coach: assists students but requires student review.
- Progression Matching Agent: suggests university/programme shortlist from verified academic record and preferences.
- Application Completeness Agent: checks missing documents/deadlines.
- Accommodation/Arrival Assistant: organises approved resources and support tasks.
- Sponsorship Intelligence Agent: consumes Sponsor SaaS/API results and explains evidence; does not promise visa outcomes.

Human careers/progression staff remain responsible for advice and partner case management.
