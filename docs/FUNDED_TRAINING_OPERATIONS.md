# Funded Training & Partner Delivery

## Delivery model
UniPathway can combine LMS/digital theory, local verified practical businesses and an optional Islamabad residential block. However, each funder/tender may impose its own delivery-location, lab/workshop, trainer, attendance and practical-hour rules. The cohort configuration must therefore store the exact contract requirements and must not assume partner-site/hybrid delivery is accepted.

## Funding sources
Track federal NAVTTC/PMYSDP/impact-bond procurements, provincial skills funds such as PSDF, BISP-linked funded quotas, donor/NGO programmes, employer cost-sharing and international-employer training.

## CRM lifecycle
WATCH -> QUALIFY -> BID -> SUBMIT -> AWARD -> FUNDED COHORT -> ELIGIBILITY/KYC -> TRAINING -> PRACTICAL -> ASSESSMENT -> COMPLETION -> EMPLOYMENT/OUTCOME -> EVIDENCE PACK -> CLAIM -> PAYMENT.

## Evidence
For every funded learner preserve identity/eligibility verification, attendance, LMS engagement, practical hours, assessments, completion/certification, placement/employment and employer evidence as required by the contract.

## Payment models
Student-paid, employer-paid, fully funded, part-funded, voucher/scholarship, cost-share and outcome-based. Student contribution and sponsor contribution are separate ledger values.

## Practical partners
Maintain verified partner agreements, facilities, supervisors, capacity, insurance/safety evidence and practical scope. Never assign a learner to a partner whose agreement does not cover the course competency.
