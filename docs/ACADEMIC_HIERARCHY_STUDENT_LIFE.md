# Academic Hierarchy & Student Life

## Academic hierarchy
Tenant/Centre -> School -> Department/Subject Area -> Programme/Course -> Academic Year -> Level -> Cohort -> Module -> Class.

A student placement records the student's school, department where applicable, programme, year, level and cohort. This supports progression between years/levels without losing historical placement.

Suggested launch schools:
- School of Business & Management
- School of Computing & Technology
- School of Accounting & Finance
- School of Health & Social Care
- School of Logistics & Supply Chain

Departments sit below schools where scale warrants it. Example: Computing & Technology -> Software & Web; Cyber & Networks; Data & AI.

## Student Life
Student dashboard gets a dedicated Student Life area. Activities can be centre-wide, school or department based:
- Debate Society
- Technology / Coding / Robotics Labs
- Sports clubs and teams
- Chess Club
- Literature / Book / Writing Society
- Entrepreneurship / Innovation Society
- Arts / media
- Volunteering/community activity
- academic subject societies

Each activity has a staff lead, membership, capacity, events and achievements.

## Competitions
Scopes:
- intra-school
- inter-school
- inter-department
- inter-centre
- inter-university
- external
- UniPathway-sponsored

Support individual/team entries, rules, registration deadlines, teams, participants, results, placing and sponsor information.

Examples: debate championship, hackathon, coding challenge, chess tournament, football/cricket/futsal/badminton/table-tennis events, business case challenge, accounting challenge, literature/writing competition, entrepreneurship pitch competition.

## External and sponsored events
UniPathway can host/sponsor competitions open to other universities/colleges. Store host, venue, participating institutions, sponsorship, safeguarding/event risk documentation and results. Commercial sponsorship is separate from academic decisions.

## Recognition
Student activity/competition history can feed a verified co-curricular transcript/profile, badges/awards and careers portfolio, while remaining separate from regulated academic grades unless a programme explicitly gives approved academic credit.
