# UniPathway Course Directory Strategy

## Core academic spine
1. OTHM regulated pathways
2. Qualifi regulated pathways
3. IAB / accounting-business routes where approved
4. ACCA teaching/support when the required centre/status arrangements are in place

These remain clearly distinguished from UniPathway short courses and external vocational routes. Never market a UniPathway certificate as an externally regulated qualification unless that exact course/centre arrangement is approved.

## Employer Academy
B2B short courses that employers can buy for cohorts, subsidise, voucher or recommend to employees:
- Food safety / food hygiene
- Workplace English
- IELTS/PTE preparation
- Excel, Power BI, data literacy
- AI for office/productivity
- Cyber awareness
- Customer service
- Supervisory/management skills
- Digital marketing/e-commerce
- Health & safety awareness
- First-aid awareness versus externally certified first aid (must be labelled correctly)
- sector-specific bespoke training

Delivery: online, UniPathway centre, employer site or blended.

## Digital & Technology
Full-stack development, web/mobile, networking, cloud, cyber security, data analytics, AI/ML, Power BI, office productivity, e-commerce, digital marketing, graphic design, animation, CAD/BIM, fibre optics and related digital trades.

## Hospitality & Food
Food safety, chef/cook, bakery & pastry, barista, fast-food operations, food & beverage service, front office, housekeeping, event management, food processing/preservation and packaging/quality.

## Fashion, Textile & Creative
Sewing, tailoring, fashion design/dress making, pattern making, jewellery, interior design, textile/manufacturing skills and digital design.

## Construction & Building Trades
Carpentry, masonry/bricklaying, plumbing, electrician/electrical, HVACR, welding, painting, surveying, BIM/CAD, site supervision and equipment operation. Practical competence should be delivered/assessed in suitable workshops or through verified partners/employers.

## Automotive & Mechanical
Automotive technician, auto electrical/AC, motorcycle/vehicle maintenance, heavy machinery mechanic, farm/tractor mechanic and related workshop-based skills.

## Agriculture & Rural Enterprise
Agribusiness, farm management, crop/horticulture, livestock, poultry, dairy, fish farming, honey-bee farming, irrigation, farm machinery, food processing and agri entrepreneurship. Combine online theory with local practical partners where needed.

## Languages & Employability
English proficiency, IELTS/PTE preparation, workplace English, academic English, communication, interview skills, CV skills and selected foreign-language programmes.

## Delivery-partner model
For plumbing, electrical, mechanics, masonry, carpentry, agriculture and other practical trades, UniPathway can partner with verified companies/workshops/farms. The LMS manages theory, attendance, competencies, practical logs, employer supervisor evidence and completion; the practical site provides real equipment/workplace exposure.

## Pakistan opportunity
NAVTTC currently publishes a large catalogue of qualifications/short courses with competency standards, curricula and lesson plans across IT, agriculture, hospitality, construction and other sectors. Examples currently published include English Proficiency (IELTS/PTE), Full Stack Development, Fashion Designing & Dress Making, Chef/Cook, Carpenter, Mason, automotive, Agribusiness and Fish Farming. These are strong reference points, but UniPathway must obtain the appropriate approval/partnership before representing a course as NAVTTC/NVQF certified.

## Catalogue UX
Filters: School/sector, qualification route, online/centre/partner-site, city, duration, level, employer-funded, practical required, start date and price.
Course card clearly displays: Award/Certificate type, delivery mode, duration, practical requirement, accreditation/approval status, progression/career use and fees.
