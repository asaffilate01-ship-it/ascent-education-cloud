# Academic Integrity Engine

## Rule
The system may detect and organise evidence of plagiarism, undeclared/prohibited AI use and other authenticity concerns. It must never automatically decide that a student cheated.

## Per-assessment AI policy
Every assignment has a controlled AI-use rule: prohibited; research-only; permitted with declaration; permitted; or declaration required. Students see the rule before submission and complete an AI-use declaration where required.

## Evidence
Ingest and preserve independently:
- similarity report and matched-source detail
- AI-writing indicator, labelled probabilistic/not proof
- citation/source verification
- suspicious or unverifiable references
- writing/style change signals across the student's own work
- document metadata/history where lawfully available
- submission/resubmission history
- marker/IQA manual observations
- authenticity-viva evidence

## Triage
Integrity Agent summarises signals and recommends no concern / human review / student discussion / formal policy review. It cannot create a misconduct finding.

## Human process
Marker may flag concern -> IQA/authorised academic reviews evidence -> student can be invited to explain -> authenticity viva where useful -> formal process under policy -> recorded decision -> appeal route.

## Authenticity viva
Short recorded LMS session with authorised staff. Questions should test whether the student can explain their own argument, sources, methodology, calculations or code. Store questions, notes, recording reference and human outcome.

## Student fairness
Similarity percentage alone is not plagiarism. AI-writing percentage alone is not proof of AI misuse. Proper quotation/citation, templates, common phrases, accessibility/language factors and false positives must be considered. The student must have an opportunity to respond before an adverse formal decision.

## Dashboard
Assessor sees integrity evidence but cannot declare misconduct from the marking screen. IQA/Quality gets a case queue, evidence timeline, student response/viva, decision and appeal status. Students see the applicable AI rule, their declaration and formal case communications appropriate to policy.
