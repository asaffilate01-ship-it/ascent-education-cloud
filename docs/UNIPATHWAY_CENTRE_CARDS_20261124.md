# Centre-issued digital identity and register lookup

The forward SQL candidate adds a private centre card register with verified active roles, current independently reviewed identity evidence and active student enrolment. A centre director must record an approved admission or appointment reference before issue. Fee policy, legal appointment and additional identity/photo checks remain separate human decisions; no card implies paid fees, employment, accreditation or physical access. Expiry is inclusive and limited to 366 days as a provisional operational safeguard. Replacing an unexpired card requires revocation first. Eligibility is rechecked on server lookup against the same identity evidence, current role and active enrolment. Cards contain no CNIC, address, date of birth or identity document. QR references are random identifiers, not authentication or attendance evidence.

The student's and lecturer's onboarding pages display the actual issued digital card only after a scoped read. The director's student registry hosts the student/lecturer issuing and revocation panel. The daily register supports text entered by a compatible QR scanner or manual reference entry; the server only returns an eligible student belonging to the assigned module's programme, and the client verifies that student is in the loaded roster. Lecturer confirms identity in person and explicitly saves the mark through the existing attendance workflow. No camera scanning, automatic entry/access control, automatic attendance, physical printing or photo capture is claimed.

Activation requires the correct production database, accepted private evidence RLS, verified enrolment policies, backup and recovery, real-role acceptance and physical identity verification procedure. `scripts/activation/unipathway-centre-id-cards-forward.sql` is a reviewed one-time candidate, not automatic migration replay. Read/write gates default off, and the frontend requires VITE_UNIPATHWAY_CENTRE_CARDS_ENABLED=true. Do not activate before all checks pass. Synthetic tests are not production acceptance. No production mutation is made by merging the source.
