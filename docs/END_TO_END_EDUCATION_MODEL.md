# End-to-End Education Operating Model Audit

## Learner/product types that must remain distinct
1. Regulated awarding-body learners: OTHM, Qualifi and other approved awarding-body programmes.
2. UniPathway/local short-course learners.
3. Employer-sponsored employees / corporate academy.
4. Years 9-12 tuition learners, including guardian/safeguarding controls.
5. Vocational/NVQF/NAVTTC-style learners with external practical partners.
6. International progression applicants/alumni.
7. Digital/technology lab learners.

Every enrolment records programme family, awarding/approval body where applicable, cohort, level/year, delivery mode, funding/payer route and required QA/assessment workflow.

## Core academic lifecycle
Course Factory/specification -> approved content/version -> cohort -> student eligibility/admission -> timetable -> lecturer assignment -> live/recorded teaching -> materials -> practical/lab -> attendance -> assignment -> specialist marker/assessor -> permitted resubmission -> final marking -> IQA/QA -> authorised grade release -> progression/completion/certificate.

Lecturers teach; specialist markers mark summative work. Qualified assessors determine vocational competence. IQA verifies according to the programme/QA plan. AI may assist but never silently replaces required human decisions.

## Delivery modes
Online: Teams + LMS content + whiteboard + interactive labs.
Centre: classroom teaching + secure attendance/access + temporary IT lab where scheduled.
Residential: two-week Islamabad centre period per academic year where applicable, with timetable, accommodation, food, attendance, trips/activities, welfare and emergency information.
Partner practical: approved employer/farm/workshop/site with logbook, evidence, supervisor sign-off and assessor verification.

## Materials
Each module has version-controlled Student Pack, Teacher Pack, Assessment Pack and QA Pack. Access is role/cohort scoped. Students see only published student/assessment material; internal answers/marking/QA remain gated.

## Timetabling
Scheduling must account for lecturer qualifications/availability, cohort/module, Teams/room, centre capacity, lab equipment, residential weeks, practical partner slots, moderator/TA need and clashes. Technology practicals can reserve a temporary laptop lab.

## Careers/progression
Careers guidance -> internships -> Pakistan/overseas jobs -> sponsor-capable opportunities where supported -> university progression -> application/admission/accommodation/pre-departure support -> commission receivable/reconciliation. University commission is financially tracked separately from academic/admissions decisions.

## Vocational examples
Agriculture/farms, mechanic/automotive, plumbing, electrical, carpentry, construction, tailoring, cooking/hospitality and technology. Theory may be online/centre; practical competence requires the approved practical environment/evidence/assessment appropriate to the programme.

## Audit acceptance
Before launch, E2E tests must cover at least one learner through each learner/product type above, including role permissions, finance, materials, attendance, assessment/QA and completion/progression.
