# Scholarships, Funding Applications & Partner Ecosystem

## UniPathway-funded places
When financially sustainable, UniPathway can create its own scholarship/sponsorship funds. Each fund has an annual budget, committed amount, eligibility criteria and dates. Awards can be full or partial. Finance approval and conflict controls are required.

## Student funding application
The LMS should show only funding routes the learner appears eligible for, then collect the exact evidence required by that funder. Typical fields can include identity/KYC, domicile/residence, age, education, employment/income status, gender or target-group eligibility where lawful/relevant, course/trade, prior funding, declarations and supporting documents. Requirements are configuration-driven per opportunity, not hard-coded globally.

Workflow: DISCOVER -> ELIGIBILITY CHECK -> APPLY -> DOCUMENTS -> VERIFY -> APPROVE/WAITLIST/REJECT -> FUNDED COHORT -> TRAINING -> OUTCOME.

## Partner ecosystem
Do not limit partnerships to blue-chip companies. Maintain a national directory across enterprise, medium, small and micro businesses.

Enterprise targets can include major employers in technology, banking, FMCG, energy, textiles, engineering, logistics, hospitality and food delivery. Named organisations are prospects until an agreement exists.

SMEs/local partners are especially important for practical delivery: garages, workshops, farms, dairies, poultry/fish farms, restaurants/hotels, bakeries, construction contractors, electricians, plumbers, carpenters, textile units, software houses, call centres/BPOs and retailers.

## Partner due diligence
Before practical/student placement: legal identity/registration, NTN where applicable, address/site inspection, responsible contact, facilities/equipment, qualified supervisor, H&S/fire/first aid, safeguarding, insurance where applicable, capacity, practical competencies covered, attendance/evidence method, incident process, data-sharing agreement, complaints route and periodic review.

## Engagement types
Internships, jobs, practical training, corporate learning, sponsorship/scholarships, competitions, guest lectures, equipment/venue sponsorship and other collaborations.

## Employer value proposition
Employers can train existing staff, recruit interns/graduates, co-design training, host practical learning, sponsor cohorts or competitions, and access verified talent with consent.

## Funding evidence
Never promise funding. Eligibility and required documents vary by scheme and contract. Store the source, version/date, required evidence, human verification and decision trail for each application.
