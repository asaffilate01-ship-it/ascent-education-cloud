# Maintaining international study and student visa guidance

- Public directory: `/international-study`. Country copy and primary links: `src/lib/internationalStudy.ts`. The country guide links back to the separate academic route. For other EU countries use `/europe` for education research and the European Commission migration portal to find national instructions.
- Keep copy specific to ordinary higher-education study from Pakistan. Separate admissions, language tests, funding evidence, and permissions. Do not imply automatic acceptance, approved advisers, paid help, permitted employment, or an immigration outcome. Do not request identity or financial documents on a public guide or generic enquiry.
- Check the destination authority (and its Pakistan mission where relevant) before editing. Use dated transitions by application date; do not silently convert figures to current costs. Review after authority updates and before the next intake. Update the source-check date only when the linked country guides have been rechecked. Do not publish unsourced fees, conversion examples, or appointment availability.
- UK numerical guidance lives in the existing academic route `/uk#student-visa-costs`; the country guide and blog link there. The UK course and evidence copy distinguishes CAS, maintenance, the outside-UK visa fee, and health surcharge. Check the separate Graduate visa rules independently. Keep TOEFL iBT enquiry-only until the language team confirms the programme, schedule and pricing.
- For new country guides, add canonical SEO entries in `src/data/public-seo.json`, regenerate `public/sitemap.xml` with `node scripts/build-public-seo.mjs --sitemap-only`, and extend the public browser coverage (country selection, official links, and return to academic information).
