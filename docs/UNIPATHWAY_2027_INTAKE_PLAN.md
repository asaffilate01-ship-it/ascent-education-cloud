# UniPathway 2027 intake plan — 27 November 2026

Owner-supplied plan, not a verified open intake, regulatory approval, job offer or timetable.

## January 2027
- Classes 9–12 supplementary tuition: technology/computer science, commerce/accounting, biology, mathematics, chemistry, physics and English, with Pakistan board and Cambridge/Edexcel preparation where staffed.
- IELTS Academic and General Training preparation.
- Technology skills and certification preparation: AI, generative AI, Python and web coding, cloud computing, databases/SQL, Microsoft, AWS and Cisco exam preparation.
- Accountancy preparation for ACCA, including appropriate papers at Applied Knowledge and Applied Skills levels, subject to confirmed teaching capacity and exam eligibility.

All tutors and lecturers are part-time. Online, hybrid and in-class delivery in Jhelum depends on approved appointments, room capacity and agreed class times. Students may register interest at /contact; recruitment enquiries go to jobs@unipathway.pk. No mass email has been sent.

## Later in 2027
Prioritise Level 3, 4 and 5 qualifications in technology/computing, business management and accounting/finance. Verify the exact qualification in the Ofqual register and secure awarding-body and centre delivery approval before advertising enrolment. Other catalogue titles are future research options. The catalogue labels do not modify existing enrolments, qualification awards or database programme status.

Academic progression: UK Level 6 degree top-up or destination-specific advanced entry in the UK, Australia, Canada, Germany or USA depends on written admission/credit recognition by the university. The UK RQF levels are not automatically the same as local year/level or degree systems. University awards and regulated diplomas are distinct.

## Immigration information
Offer official information and appropriate qualified/authorised adviser referrals for study, post-study, work, family/spouse/marriage and visit routes, globally on enquiry. Do not claim individual immigration advice authorisation, guaranteed credit transfer, visa approval, employment, work sponsorship or settlement. Confirm country-specific post-study programme duration, attendance, institution and current rules before enrolment. UK Graduate route has a scheduled change from 1 January 2027 and is not itself a settlement route.
